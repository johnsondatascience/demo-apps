Step 01

Your number sets the level

The anchor fixes today’s level; the dials fix drift, volatility and a seasonal factor. Together they define a generative process — not a spreadsheet row.

Step 02

Five thousand futures run

Each path walks forward in log space with correlated shocks (AR-1, φ = 0.35), so a bad month makes the next one likelier to be bad — the way real months behave.

Step 03

The answer is a distribution

Those paths get summarized into quantiles and a probability you can act on. Same discipline on the lift test, where the posterior is exact rather than simulated.
